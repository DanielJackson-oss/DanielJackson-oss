About Me

Why Web Development?

I decided to take this web development course because I wanted to gain a better understanding of the way websites are developed and learn what really goes on behind the scenes. The internet is such a huge part of our daily lives, and I was curious about how it all works.

I don't know yet exactly what I want to do with these skills, but I want to figure it out. I'm keeping an open mind and exploring different possibilities as I continue to learn. Who knows where this journey will take me?
