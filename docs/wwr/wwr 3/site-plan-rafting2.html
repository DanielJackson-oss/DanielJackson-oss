Overview

Purpose

The purpose of the Dry Oar Rafting website is to get people excited about the thrill of white water rafting with Dry Oar. It showcases the different trips and locations, explains how to stay safe, and makes it easy to book an adventure. The site is intended to help visitors plan their rafting experience and take action to reserve a trip.

Audience

The users of the Dry Oar Rafting website would include adults and families that go on outdoor adventures. They would want fun, yet safe, trips that have clear information about where, how much, and how to book a trip. This would mostly be searched on the phone or computer when planning a trip.
